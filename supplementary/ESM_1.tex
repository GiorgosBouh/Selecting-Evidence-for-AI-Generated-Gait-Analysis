Secondary semantic audit & 4,517 blinded LLM-judge records for Clinical Gait Signals and Health\&Gait. Mean usefulness was 3.447 for FR and 3.383 for TGR; winner counts were FR 1,955, TGR 1,775, tie 787 & Semantic usefulness did not automatically improve with TGR, again showing that the best method depends on the evidence \\

\section*{Supplementary Prompt and Claim-Audit Artifacts}

The exact generation prompts, JSON output schema, deterministic support-checking rules, and representative claim-level audit examples are provided as repository artifacts rather than repeated in full here. This keeps the supplementary PDF readable while preserving reproducibility.

The recommended repository files are:

\begin{itemize}
\item \path|docs/reproducibility/generation_prompts_and_schema.md| and \path|.json| for the exact prompt templates, output schema, and generation settings.
\item \path|docs/reproducibility/support_checking_rules.md| for deterministic claim-support and metric rules.
\item \path|docs/reproducibility/claim_audit_examples.json| and \path|.csv| for representative case-feature, retrieved-context, generated-claim, and support-label examples.
\end{itemize}

Full generated outputs and claim-level tables are better archived as repository or DOI-backed data files because they are too large for a readable supplementary PDF. The reproducibility folder also documents the OGR graph construction, the primary versus audit prompt scope, and the deterministic quality-score caveat. The primary-prompt OGR rerun used for the final main comparison is archived as \path|outputs/ontology_graph_full_primary_prompt_2026-07-02.jsonl|.
